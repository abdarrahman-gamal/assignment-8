Pan-Seared Teriyaki Chicken Bowl

Crispy-edged chicken thighs coated in a glossy ginger-garlic glaze, served over steamed rice and greens.

  • 1 400g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized strips
  • 2 1/2 cup teriyaki sauce
  • 3 2 cups warm cooked white rice
  • 4 1 broccoli crown, chopped into small florets
  • 5 1 large carrot, sliced into thin matchsticks
  • 6 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 7 2 green onions, sliced thin on a bias
  • 8 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
1

Set a heavy skillet or wok over high heat with the sesame oil. Once it shimmers, drop in the chicken and sear untouched for 3 to 4 minutes so the edges caramelize, then toss until cooked through.

2

Turn the burner down to medium-low and pour in the teriyaki sauce. Let it simmer gently for 4–5 minutes until it thickens into a syrup that coats the back of a spoon.

3

In the meantime, steam the broccoli and carrots for roughly 3 minutes until vibrant and tender-crisp. Don't overcook them—they should retain some bite.

4

Ladle warm rice into two shallow bowls.

5

Arrange the sticky glazed chicken and steamed vegetables across the rice, then drizzle any excess sauce left in the skillet over everything.

6

Scatter over the sliced scallions and sesame seeds, and bring straight to the table.

Stick with boneless thighs—breast meat dries out before the sauce reduces down properly.

Bottled teriyaki running too sweet? Whisk in a grating of fresh ginger and a teaspoon of rice vinegar right in the skillet.

Throw shelled edamame right into the steamer alongside the carrots to get more color without washing another pot.

If you're meal prepping, pack the rice separately from the sauce so you don't end up with soggy grains tomorrow.
